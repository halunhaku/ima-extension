Retrieval Is Not Memory

every.to· 14:32· 842 words· 3 images Auto

Every assistant you used this year is built on the same quiet bet: that the right paragraph, fetched at the right moment, can stand in for knowing anything.

It is a good bet, and it changed what reading feels like. A page stops being something you finish and becomes something you might someday quote. You skim faster, because a summary is always one ask away, and you remember less, because the summary was never yours.

What retrieval actually does

Retrieval moves the question from what do you know to what can you find. That is a real capability, not a party trick — but it is a capability of the archive, not of the reader.

Memory is what survives the search. Retrieval is what shows up in it.

Keep the source offsets. When a chunk answers a question, the useful part of the answer is usually the sentence around it, and chunk_id alone will not get you back there.

answer = model.ask(question, context=retrieve(question))

Reading as a candidate pool

  • Chunk at paragraph boundaries, not at character counts.
  • Keep the source offsets next to every chunk.
  • Fetch three, not thirty.

A capture that is honest about what it could not extract is worth more than one that is confident about what it guessed.

改了什么：品牌行与页面标题走衬线，抓取模式徽标从胶囊改成 2px 方角纸签；元信息用 · 分隔，不再是一排灰胶囊；标签页去掉滑动药丸与阴影，改下划线索引；正文卡片 1px 细线框、无阴影、无玻璃。按钮可点：Copy 会换成 Copied + 对勾，图标按钮（URL / 标题 / 下载）点完短暂显示对勾。注意：现有弹窗实宽 560px，点右上角可在 360/560 间对照。
